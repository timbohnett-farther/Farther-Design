# Official client disclosure

Single source of truth for Farther's required disclosure on all client-facing material. Use it verbatim: no edits, trims, paraphrases or reordering. Updated September 2026. Applied to all 77 library pieces with legacy wording and to the Client Report and Client Deck wireframes (Sept 26, 2026).

- Heading: **Important disclosures**
- Two approved versions: **full** and **condensed** (both below). Use either verbatim; never mix or edit them.
- Code: `assets/compliance/disclosure.js` (`FartherDisclosure.mount(el, {variant, length})`, or `<div data-farther-disclosure="print" data-length="condensed"></div>`)
- Card: `preview/disclosure.html` (Brand group)

## Which version

- **Full:** multi-page reports, the Client Review Package (client report and appendix), education guides, benefits guides, and anything that discusses new deposits, consolidations or rollovers (the full version carries the wrap-fee conflict-of-interest paragraph).
- **Condensed:** one-pagers, data fact sheets, slide decks, the Client Education library one-pagers, and other space-limited pieces.

## Topic-specific disclosures

The official text is the baseline, not a ceiling. Under the SEC Marketing Rule (Rule 206(4)-1) and general antifraud principles, a piece must also carry the risk disclosures its content makes material. Put them in one paragraph directly after the official text, same style:

- **Index or historical market data:** index is unmanaged, has no fees, can't be invested in directly, isn't representative of any Farther strategy; past performance is not indicative of future results; data sources.
- **Hypothetical or projected results:** hypothetical performance has inherent limitations; no client is likely to achieve the results shown; the key assumptions.
- **Tax figures and limits:** figures reflect law as of publication and change; confirm current limits with a tax advisor or irs.gov.
- **Insurance and annuities:** guarantees depend on the carrier's claims-paying ability; fees, surrender charges, underwriting.
- **Alternatives, private markets, real assets:** illiquidity, possible loss of the entire investment, accredited or qualified investors only.
- **Trusts, estate, legal topics:** Farther does not practice law; documents must be drafted by a qualified attorney.

Don't repeat anything the official text already covers (registration, not advice, loss of principal, Form ADV and CRS, consult professionals).

## Placement

- **Reports, guides, one-pagers, fact sheets:** at the end of the last page, under the heading. If the last page has no room, give it its own final page. Report-specific source notes can sit above it but never replace it.
- **Slide decks:** the final slide, at 24px minimum type.
- **Multi-document packages (e.g. Client Review Package):** at the end of the client report and again in the appendix.
- **Internal-only material** (advisor briefs): not required, but must be labeled internal.
- **Minimum type:** 6.3pt in print, 24px on 1920×1080 slides. Ink #5B6A71 on light backgrounds (5.9:1 on white), #C9C9C9 on navy #31465C; never below 4.5:1 contrast. Do not use lighter greys such as #8A8580.
