Tax-aware investing
Comparing tax-equivalent yield rather than stated yield
Comparing a municipal bond's yield directly to a taxable bond's yield is misleading, the correct comparison is the taxable bond's tax-equivalent yield, which rises with your marginal tax bracket.
How it works
A municipal bond yielding 3.5% may look lower than a taxable bond yielding 4.5%, but for a household in a high tax bracket, the municipal bond's tax-equivalent yield can exceed the taxable bond's actual yield once taxes are factored in.
Step 1
Identify the municipal bond's stated yield.
Step 2
Identify your marginal federal (and applicable state) tax bracket.
Step 3
Divide the muni yield by (1 minus your tax bracket) to calculate the tax-equivalent yield.
Step 4
Compare that tax-equivalent yield directly against taxable bond alternatives.
