Charitable planning
Front-loading charitable deductions while granting over time
Bunching charitable contributions into a donor-advised fund in a single high-income year, then granting to charities over subsequent years, can maximize the tax benefit of itemizing deductions.
How it works
Rather than giving the same amount to charity every year, which may not exceed the standard deduction threshold, bunching combines several years of intended giving into one large DAF contribution, itemizing that year, then distributing grants to charities on the donor's normal timeline afterward.
Step 1
Estimate several years of intended annual charitable giving.
Step 2
Contribute that combined amount to a donor-advised fund in a single high-income year.
Step 3
Claim the full deduction in that contribution year, exceeding the standard deduction.
Step 4
Grant to specific charities from the DAF over the following years, on the same timeline as before.
Bunching vs. Annual Giving
A household giving $10,000/year compares taking the standard deduction each year versus bunching 3 years into one DAF contribution.

Donor-Advised Fund "Bunching" · fit and reference
Who this is designed for
Households whose annual giving falls near the standard deduction threshold
Anyone with a high-income year, from a bonus, sale, or other event, suited to a larger deduction
Charitably inclined donors wanting to maximize tax benefit without changing their giving pattern
Key benefits at a glance
Maximizes the value of itemizing in a single, well-timed year
Preserves the donor's normal multi-year granting schedule
Pairs well with donating appreciated securities instead of cash
Can be timed to coincide with an unusually high-income year
Important considerations
Never itemizing
Continuing to give the same modest annual amount without itemizing, missing the deduction benefit entirely.
Cash instead of stock
Funding a DAF with cash instead of appreciated securities when both are available.
Poor timing
Failing to coordinate the bunching year with other income and deduction planning.
