Every decline on this chart ended the same way: the S&P 500 returned to — and then surpassed — its prior peak. Bar lengths are drawn to a common scale, so shorter bars mean faster round trips.
Insight 01 — Structural vs. cyclical
Systemic banking or valuation unwinds (1973, 2001, 2008) create prolonged recovery windows lasting 5–7+ years, whereas event-driven shocks (1990, 2020) rebound rapidly within months.
Insight 02 — Markets lead the economy
Stock markets consistently lead macro fundamentals — typically topping out 6–9 months prior to official recession onset and bottoming 3–6 months before economic recovery begins.
