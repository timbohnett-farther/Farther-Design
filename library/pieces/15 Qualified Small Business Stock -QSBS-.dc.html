Equity compensation
Understanding Section 1202 eligibility, holding periods, and exclusion potential
Qualified Small Business Stock (QSBS) can offer a significant federal capital gains exclusion on the sale of eligible startup or small business stock, but eligibility depends on specific requirements around the company, the stock, and the holding period.
How it works
Stock acquired directly from an eligible small C-corporation may qualify for exclusion of a substantial portion of the gain from federal capital gains tax upon sale: 50% at a 3-year hold, 75% at 4 years, and 100% at 5 or more years, subject to per-issuer limits and specific requirements that must be verified well before a sale.
Step 1
Confirm the issuing company meets QSBS eligibility requirements at the time the stock was issued.
Step 2
Hold the stock at least 3 years from issuance; the exclusion percentage rises at 3, 4, and 5 years.
Step 3
At sale, calculate the eligible exclusion amount, subject to per-issuer limits.
Step 4
Report the exclusion properly on the applicable tax forms.

Qualified Small Business Stock (QSBS) · fit and reference
Who this is designed for
Founders and early employees holding stock in an eligible startup or small business
Investors who directly acquired stock from an eligible small C-corporation
Anyone approaching the five-year holding period on potentially qualifying stock
Key benefits at a glance
Can exclude a substantial portion of the gain from federal capital gains tax
Rewards early, long-term investment in qualifying small businesses
Exclusion applies per issuer, potentially across multiple qualifying investments
Can be paired with gifting strategies to multiply exclusion across family members
Important considerations
Assumed eligibility
Assuming stock qualifies without verifying the issuing company met all requirements at issuance.
Early sale
Selling before the 3-year mark, forfeiting the exclusion entirely.
Poor documentation
Failing to properly document QSBS eligibility, which can be difficult to reconstruct years later.
Quick reference
Minimum holding period
3 years, tiered to 5
Potential exclusion
Greater of $15M or 10x basis
Entity type required
Domestic C-corporation
Gross asset ceiling
~$75M at issuance
Verification timing
At issuance, not at sale
How Farther approaches this
Farther works with a household's tax and legal advisors to verify QSBS eligibility early, well before the 3, 4, and 5-year exclusion milestones, and models the potential exclusion value as a sale approaches.
Questions worth asking your advisor
01
Does my stock qualify as QSBS, and has this been verified with documentation from issuance?
02
How much of the five-year holding period remains before I could qualify for the exclusion?
03
What is the potential exclusion value given my basis and expected sale price?
