Advanced trust structures
Long-duration trust planning, generation-skipping transfer tax allocation, trustee selection, distribution standards, situs selection, and governance
A dynasty trust is designed to hold and grow family wealth across multiple generations, often for as long as applicable state law permits, using generation-skipping transfer tax exemption allocation, careful trustee selection, and defined governance to preserve the family's wealth and values over time.
How it works
Without specific planning, wealth transferred across multiple generations can be taxed at each transition, once at the parents' level and again at the grandchildren's level. A dynasty trust, properly funded with generation-skipping transfer tax exemption, can hold assets for the benefit of multiple future generations without repeated transfer taxation at each generational transition, provided it is established in a jurisdiction and structure that supports long duration.
Step 1
Select a trust situs jurisdiction that supports long or indefinite trust duration.
Step 2
Fund the trust and allocate available generation-skipping transfer tax exemption to shield it from GST tax.
Step 3
Establish clear distribution standards and governance provisions to guide trustees across multiple generations.
Step 4
Select trustees, and often a trust protector, capable of administering the trust responsibly over a very long time horizon.
