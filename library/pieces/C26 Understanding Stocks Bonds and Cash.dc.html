Investing fundamentals
Three roles, not three competitors
Stocks are partial ownership in a company, offering growth with higher volatility. Bonds are loans to a government or company that pay interest, generally with more stability. Cash and equivalents offer safety and liquidity with minimal growth.
How it works
Each behaves differently under the same economic conditions. No single class is best; the right portfolio blends all three according to horizon and the need for growth, income, or stability.
Step 1
Match each goal to the class whose role fits its timeline.
Step 2
Size the stock allocation to the volatility you can hold through.
Step 3
Use bonds for stability and income, understanding they can fall when rates rise.
Step 4
Hold only the cash you need, since safety comes at the cost of inflation.
