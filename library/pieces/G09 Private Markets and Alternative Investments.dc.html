Illustrative investor
A compelling opportunity can still be a poor portfolio fit
The situation
An investor qualifies for a private fund and likes the strategy. The household also owns rental property, expects college expenses, and has outstanding commitments to two prior funds.
Questions to coordinate
01What portfolio role would the new fund serve?
02How much household wealth is already illiquid?
03What if prior funds distribute later than expected?
04Can future calls be funded without selling public assets under stress?
05Does the manager improve diversification or add overlap?
