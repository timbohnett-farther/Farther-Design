Three things to remember
Manage retirement taxes as a lifetime system
1Map every income source by tax character, timing, and purpose
2Use lower-income windows deliberately, and measure effects on Social Security, Medicare, state tax, and future RMDs
3Revisit annually and after retirement, claiming, RMD, relocation, health, or family changes
Self-assessment
Do you know which accounts will fund the first five years of retirement?
Have you modeled taxes across several years rather than one return?
Are conversions, gains, Medicare, RMDs, giving, and survivor taxes evaluated together?
Are withholding and estimated payments aligned with the income plan?
