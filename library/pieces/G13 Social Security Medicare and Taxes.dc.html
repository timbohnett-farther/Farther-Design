Topic-specific notes
For educational purposes only. This presentation is not individualized investment, tax, legal, insurance, or accounting advice. Full retirement age is 67 for people born in 1960 or later. Medicare’s initial enrollment period generally spans seven months; COBRA does not extend the Part B special enrollment period. Medigap open enrollment generally lasts six months from Part B enrollment at 65 or older. Income-related Medicare premiums use a two-year lookback. Thresholds, limits, and rules change and should be confirmed for the applicable year before each use.
