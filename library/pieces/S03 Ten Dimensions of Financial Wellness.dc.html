How the dimensions work together
Why coordination is the point
Each dimension can be handled alone, and most households end up doing exactly that: a CPA for tax, an attorney for estate documents, a broker for investments, an agent for insurance. The cost is rarely a bad decision in any one area — it is decisions that are individually reasonable and collectively incoherent.
