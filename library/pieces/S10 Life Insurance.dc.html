Life insurance · purpose and benefits
The purpose of life insurance
If anyone depends on your income, protecting it becomes a top priority
Life insurance is designed to pay out some amount at the end of the policyholder's life. The benefit at death should be sufficient to cover the policyholder's obligations, such as debt and share of anticipated expenses.
Potential benefits of life insurance
Replaces income that would have been used to pay mortgage and other debt, child care or elder care, and educational expenses
Protects lifestyle
Covers funeral costs
Provides for family members with long-term care needs
Safeguards assets by covering estate taxes

Choosing a structure and sizing coverage
Where each structure fits
Structure
Use case
Term
Best policy in the vast majority of cases. Low cost, pure life insurance ensures you do not pay extra fees for investment management
Whole
Families with taxable estates or other large end-of-life costs may benefit from a policy that covers anticipated estate taxes. The policy usually can be borrowed against and assets grow tax-deferred, which can offset the higher cost of premiums and locked-up assets when kept for very long periods of time
Universal
Families with highly uncertain financial situations that need a guaranteed death benefit
How much life insurance do you need?
We generally recommend buying enough term life insurance to protect the income your family counts on. We will help you determine your need by asking a few questions to understand your obligations and project your expenses. We will then calculate the present value of those needs to determine the coverage you need.
