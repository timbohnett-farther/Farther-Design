Expenses
Calculating the amount of money you need to live off of for today and for tomorrow is a complex problem to solve. Our scenario-based financial planning enables us to input multiple factors and run an analysis of how these elements will affect you positively or negatively. Our objective is to take the guesswork out of what retirement will look like, by comparing and contrasting items including:
Can we buy that beach house?
How will our assets be allocated, once we are retired?
What if we need long-term care?

Estate, allocation, income, and life events
The questions we help you answer
Area
What we work through
Estate planning
Do I have a will? What will my spouse get, and will they be supported? How much will I pay in taxes? How will my assets transfer?
Asset allocation
What if healthcare expenses rise? How much will long-term care cost? Will I require assisted living, or help from adult children?
Income
If I retire a year earlier or later, what does that look like? How much do I need? What is a Roth conversion, and should I do one? Where do I pull taxes from first?
Life events
When do you take Social Security benefits? What is on the Medicare checklist? Should you look into supplemental insurance? What costs come with doctor visits, long-term care, and medication?
