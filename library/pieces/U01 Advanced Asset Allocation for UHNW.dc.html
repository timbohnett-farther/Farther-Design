Portfolio construction
Your portfolio is not your allocation
At $10 million and above, the advised account is usually a minority of the household. The operating business, vested equity compensation, directly held real estate, and carried interest all carry risk, and most of it is equity risk. A portfolio that reads 60/40 on a statement can sit inside a household running above 80% equity exposure.
Advanced allocation starts by measuring exposure across the full position, then uses the liquid sleeve deliberately, as a counterweight rather than a standalone portfolio. A household that owns index funds, employer stock, and an operating company may be making the same bet three times without ever deciding to.

Three points carry the piece
Your allocation is the sum of everything you own, not the mix inside one account. Passive index exposure has become a concentrated position in its own right. And the liquid portfolio earns its keep by counterweighting the illiquid side, which is a different job than maximizing its own return.
How Farther approaches this
Most allocation advice stops at the edge of what a firm custodies, because that is what the firm is paid on. Farther is a fiduciary, fee-based advisor, which removes that incentive. The Intelligent Wealth Platform holds your full position in one dashboard, including assets Farther does not manage, so exposure is measured across the business, the equity compensation, and the portfolio together.
What that looks like in practice
Setting the liquid allocation against total equity exposure rather than in isolation
Identifying overlap between concentrated positions and index holdings
Sequencing harvested losses against recognized gains in multi-year reduction plans
Sourcing private market strategies through open architecture, with no commission conflict
Rebalancing on a schedule that accounts for assets you cannot trade
