Business transitions
The multiple is set years before the sale
Per an Exit Planning Institute analysis of 312 closed lower middle market transactions, companies with a documented succession plan sold at a median 6.8 times EBITDA. Matched peers without one sold at 5.9 times. On $4 million of earnings that difference is $3.6 million, and it was decided years before either company received an offer.

Three points carry the piece
Buyers price key-person risk as a deduction from the multiple, which means the value of your company is partly a function of how well it runs without you. Transfer planning must be completed before a letter of intent exists, because a negotiated price eliminates the valuation discount that makes the transfer efficient. And roughly half of exits are not chosen by the owner, which makes preparation a contingency plan as much as a value strategy.
How Farther approaches this
An exit is the moment when business advice, tax planning, estate planning, and investment management become the same conversation, and most owners have four advisors who have never met. Farther earns no transaction-based compensation on a business sale, so there is no incentive to encourage one or discourage one.
What that looks like in practice
Modeling after-tax proceeds under each exit route before you engage a banker
Sequencing gifts and trust funding well ahead of any letter of intent, while discounts remain available
Confirming qualified small business stock eligibility batch by batch rather than assuming it
Coordinating attorney, CPA, and banker so the estate plan and the deal structure do not fight each other
Building the post-sale plan before the sale, because a liquid balance sheet is a different problem
