Risk management
The position that built your wealth can also undo it
Concentration is how most large fortunes are made, and how a meaningful share of them are unmade. You do not have to choose between paying a large tax bill today and carrying that risk indefinitely. There is a set of tools between those two answers, and each trades something specific for something specific.

She addressed the entire position for roughly 9% of what an outright sale would have cost in tax, kept meaningful upside on half of it, and put a floor under the rest.
What the table does not show
The exchange fund carries a lock-up measured in years and her original basis travels with her, so the gain is deferred rather than forgiven. The collar caps her upside on that portion. The gifted shares are gone permanently, and the deduction is subject to the 30% of income limit for appreciated property and the 0.5% floor that took effect in 2026. Every one of these tools trades something. The work is deciding which trade you are willing to make.

Three points carry the piece
The historical failure rate of individual stocks is far higher than most holders assume, and profitability at the peak did not protect the companies that failed. The choice is not binary, because there is a toolkit between selling everything and doing nothing. And every tool in that kit trades one thing for another, so the right question is which trade fits your situation rather than which strategy is best.
How Farther approaches this
Concentrated position advice is often a product conversation, because most of these tools are manufactured by someone paid when you use them. Farther is fee-based with open architecture, so a collar, an exchange fund, and a staged sale plan are compared on their merits rather than on who distributes them.
What that looks like in practice
Measuring the position as a share of your total balance sheet, not just your portfolio
Running direct indexing on the diversified sleeve so harvested losses are waiting when you sell
Comparing hedging, exchange, charitable, and sale strategies with the after-tax result of each
Structuring hedges with enough distance to stay clear of constructive sale treatment
Coordinating with counsel on trading windows, insider restrictions, and pre-arranged trading plans
