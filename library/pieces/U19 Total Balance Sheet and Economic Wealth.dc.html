Wealth structure
The wealth your statements do not show
The federal estate and gift tax exemption is now $15 million per person, $30 million for a couple, and permanent. For the first time in years, families can plan against a fixed target instead of a countdown. The families who use it well will be the ones who can answer a simpler question first: what is actually on the balance sheet?
A total balance sheet lists everything you own and everything you owe, not only what sits at a custodian. Above roughly $10 million of net worth, the custodial statement captures a minority of the real number. Standard practice adds two entries that appear on no statement: human capital, the present value of your remaining career earnings, and the present value of your committed future spending.

What a complete picture gives you
A real federal exposure number. Every dollar above $30 million for a married couple is taxed at 40%.
Visibility into assets inside your estate but outside your plan. A personally owned policy is includable at full death benefit value.
An answer on IRC §6166. Where a closely held business exceeds 35% of the adjusted gross estate, the attributable federal tax may be spread over as long as 14 years.
Your true equity concentration, once an operating business sits beside a portfolio averaging 51% public equity
A funding plan for future obligations that protects your existing low-rate debt

Three points carry the piece
Your real balance sheet is several times what your statements show. The permanent exemption traded a deadline for latitude, and latitude only helps families who know their number. And the highest-value work here is documentation before it is strategy.
How Farther approaches this
Most wealth management is built around the assets a firm happens to custody, because that is what the firm is paid on. The Intelligent Wealth Platform brings your full position into a single dashboard, including holdings Farther does not manage, so the business, the property, and the deferred compensation sit next to the portfolio rather than in separate files.
