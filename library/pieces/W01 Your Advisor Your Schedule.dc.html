Employee education guide · getting started
Start with one conversation
You do not need every answer before you ask for help
Maybe you are deciding how much to save, wondering what to do with an old 401(k), managing debt, preparing for a family change, or simply asking: am I on the right path? Through your workplace, you have access to a dedicated Farther advisor who can help you think through the next decision — meeting virtually, on a schedule that works for you, with the same advisor over time.
What to expect
1. Choose a time
Schedule a video or phone conversation around work, family, travel, and real life.
2. Meet your advisor
Talk with a dedicated advisor who learns what matters to you — not a new person every time.
3. Leave with a next step
Get practical next steps, whether saving more, organizing accounts, addressing debt, or confirming what already works.
Meet virtually. Plan personally.
Video or phone
Choose the format that feels most comfortable.
A consistent relationship
Your dedicated advisor stays connected to your goals and prior conversations.
Include a partner
A spouse or partner can join from a different location when helpful.
Your first conversation is about you and the question in front of you. You are not required to move an account, make a decision during the meeting, or commit to anything beyond the next step that makes sense.
