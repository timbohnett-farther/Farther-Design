The client problem

Why affluent families outgrow traditional wealth management

As wealth grows, complexity grows with it. Accounts multiply. Entities stack up. Advisors operate in silos. Important decisions get made without the full picture.

Families with wealth often find themselves managing

  • Multiple advisors and institutions
  • Trusts, LLCs, partnerships, and business interests
  • Concentrated positions and low-basis assets
  • Real estate, private investments, and lending needs
  • Tax, estate, and family decisions that affect one another

The result is not a lack of advice. It is a lack of coordination.

Our services · Tax strategy

Improving what you keep, not just what you earn

Why it matters to clients

Taxes are one of the largest lifetime drags on wealth. For affluent families, tax exposure often comes from several directions at once, including investment gains, business income, real estate activity, trust structures, and future estate transfers.

Why Farther does it differently

Farther treats tax strategy as an ongoing part of wealth management, not a once-a-year exercise. Investment decisions, liquidity events, charitable planning, and distribution strategy are reviewed with tax impact in mind before action is taken.

Why that matters

Clients are better positioned to keep more of what they build over time. They also benefit from stronger coordination with CPAs and other outside professionals, which can make the overall planning process more proactive and less reactive.

Helping preserve wealth across generations

Why it matters to clients

Estate planning affects control, flexibility, family outcomes, and tax efficiency. Families with complex estates, closely held businesses, or multi-generational priorities often need more than documents in place. They need a plan that is reviewed, understood, and coordinated over time.

Why Farther does it differently

Farther helps keep estate conversations active and connected to the rest of the wealth plan. That means bringing together the advisor, estate attorney, tax professionals, and family stakeholders so decisions are informed by the full context.

Clients can make more intentional decisions about transfer strategy, family support, charitable goals, and long-term stewardship. Good coordination can also reduce the risk that strong legal documents are undermined by weak implementation or outdated assumptions.

Our services · Alternative investments

Access where appropriate, discipline always

Why it matters to clients

Alternative investments can offer diversification, return potential, and access to opportunities outside traditional public markets. They also introduce complexity around liquidity, fees, manager selection, capital calls, valuation, and portfolio fit.

Why Farther does it differently

Farther evaluates alternatives within the context of the full wealth plan, not as standalone ideas. The emphasis is on selectivity, suitability, and disciplined allocation rather than presenting alternatives as a badge of exclusivity.

Why that matters

Clients can make more informed decisions about whether an opportunity truly belongs in the portfolio, how much exposure makes sense, and how it affects liquidity and overall risk across the family balance sheet.
