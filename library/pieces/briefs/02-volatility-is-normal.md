## Insight Callouts

INSIGHT 01 — DECLINES ARE THE ENTRY FEE
Not one of the last 46 years passed without a drop. The average was 14.2%, and the market still finished higher three years out of four. Volatility is not a sign the plan is broken. It is the price of the returns the plan depends on.

INSIGHT 02 — THE HEADLINE IS NOT THE OUTCOME
In 2020 the S&P 500 fell 34% and ended the year up 16%. In 2025 it fell 19% and ended up 16%. The worst moment of a year rarely tells you how the year ends, so decisions made at that moment tend to be expensive.
