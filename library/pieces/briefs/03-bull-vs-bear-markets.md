## Insight Callouts

INSIGHT 01 — THE MATH IS ASYMMETRIC
Bear markets have been short and sharp. Bull markets have been long and large. Since 1946 the average bear lasted about a year and took back roughly a third; the average bull ran more than five years and roughly tripled the index. Time has favored the patient owner.

INSIGHT 02 — YOU NEVER GET THE BELL
No one announced the bottom in March 2009 or in October 2022. Recoveries begin while the news is still bad. That is why a plan built to stay invested usually beats one built to react.
