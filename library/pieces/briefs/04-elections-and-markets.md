## Insight Callouts

INSIGHT 01 — THE GAP IS REAL AND MOSTLY COINCIDENTAL
Returns have been higher under Democratic presidents in every study we checked. They have also been positive under presidents of both parties in most years. Presidents inherit economies more than they create them, and markets price policy long before it passes.

INSIGHT 02 — ELECTION YEARS ARE JUST YEARS
Since 1945, election years averaged a 6.8% gain and rose four times in five. That is a little below the all-year average, and nowhere near a reason to sit out. The bigger risk is going to cash for a season and missing the recovery that follows.
