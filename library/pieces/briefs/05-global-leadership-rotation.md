## Insight Callouts

INSIGHT 01 — THE LEADER ALWAYS CHANGES
Since 1970 leadership between U.S. and international stocks has swung back and forth in long stretches. The last U.S. run lasted 204 months, the longest on record, which is exactly why it felt permanent. In 2025 international developed and emerging markets both beat the S&P 500 by more than 13 points.

INSIGHT 02 — DIVERSIFICATION IS PAID LATE
Owning what is lagging feels like a mistake for years at a time. That is the cost of admission. You cannot know when the rotation arrives, so the position has to be in place before it does.

## Quote
