## Insight Callouts

INSIGHT 01 — THE FLAT DECADE WAS REAL
From year-end 1999 to year-end 2009, the balance went from $34,756 to $31,581. Ten years, no progress. Investors who sold during that stretch locked in the loss; investors who held saw the balance grow more than eightfold over the next 16 years.

INSIGHT 02 — RECOVERIES ARE MEASURED IN YEARS, NOT DECADES
The dot-com bust took until 2006 to erase. The 2008 crisis took until 2012. The COVID crash was undone inside the same calendar year. Painful, but finite — which is exactly why your time horizon, not your nerve, should set your allocation.
