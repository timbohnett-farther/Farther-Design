## Insight Callouts

INSIGHT 01 — SMALL YIELD, ENORMOUS EFFECT
A 1% or 2% dividend looks like a rounding error next to a 20% up year. But reinvested, it buys shares that pay their own dividends, which buy more shares. Over 66 years that loop produced 6.6 times more ending wealth than price gains alone.

INSIGHT 02 — DIVIDENDS DO THEIR BEST WORK IN BAD DECADES
In the 1970s dividends supplied 73% of the S&P 500's total return. In the 2000s, when the index lost ground, dividends still paid 1.8% a year. When prices stall, the income keeps buying — which is why reinvestment matters most precisely when it feels least rewarding.
