## Insight Callouts

**INSIGHT 01 — THE LEADER KEEPS CHANGING**
Emerging markets finished dead last of nine in 2021 and seventh of nine in 2022. Then they finished first in 2025, up 34.4%. Nothing about the company or the country changed that fast. What changed was the price other people were willing to pay.

**INSIGHT 02 — THE MIDDLE IS THE POINT**
The diversified portfolio never won a single year in fifteen. It also never lost one. It ranked between third and seventh every year, and it still compounded at 6.56% while emerging markets managed 4.22%. Boring is not the price of diversification. Boring is the product.
