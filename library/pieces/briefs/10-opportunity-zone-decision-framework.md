# Opportunity Zone Investments: The Real Decision Framework

**CLIENT EDUCATION GUIDE**

## Separating tax deferral from investment underwriting

Opportunity Zone investments offer meaningful tax deferral and potential exclusion benefits, but the tax benefit should never be the primary reason to invest, the underlying investment must be underwritten on its own merits first.

## The Core Idea

An Opportunity Zone fund can defer capital gains tax and, if held long enough, provide additional tax benefits on the new investment's own appreciation. But the underlying real estate or business investment still carries all the normal investment risks, illiquidity, execution risk, and market risk, and a bad investment with a good tax wrapper is still a bad investment.

## How It Works

**STEP 1.** Identify a capital gain eligible for deferral through Opportunity Zone investment.

**STEP 2.** Underwrite the specific underlying investment, real estate or business, independent of the tax benefit.

**STEP 3.** Invest the gain into a Qualified Opportunity Fund within the required timeframe.

**STEP 4.** Track the holding period carefully, since the tax benefits depend heavily on how long the investment is held.

## Tax Wrapper vs. Investment Outcome

A $500,000 deferred gain invested in an Opportunity Zone fund illustrates how the tax benefit and investment outcome are separate questions.

| Scenario | Tax Deferral Value | Investment Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Strong underlying investment, held 10+ yrs | Deferral + potential exclusion on growth | Positive, compounding return |
| Weak underlying investment, held 10+ yrs | Deferral + potential exclusion on growth | Possible loss of principal |


*Hypothetical illustration only. The tax structure does not offset or protect against underlying investment losses.*

## Who This Is Designed For

- Investors with a recent or upcoming significant capital gain
- Households evaluating an Opportunity Zone offering presented primarily on its tax benefits
- Anyone comfortable with the illiquidity and risk of the specific underlying investment

## Key Benefits at a Glance

- Defers capital gains tax on the original gain invested
- May reduce or eliminate tax on the new investment's own appreciation if held long enough
- Can support community development-oriented investing goals
- Offers a structured way to redeploy a large gain into real assets

## Important Considerations

**Tax-first thinking.** Investing primarily for the tax deferral without adequately underwriting the underlying investment.

**Illiquidity surprise.** Underestimating the illiquidity of the investment, often locked up for many years.

**Missed deadline.** Missing the strict timeline requirements for reinvesting the eligible gain.

## Deferred Gain Value by Holding Period (Illustrative $500,000 Gain)

| Holding Period | Deferral Status | Additional Exclusion on OZ Growth |
|---|---|---|
| Less than 5 years | Gain deferred only | None |
| 5-7 years | Gain deferred only | None (historic basis step-up expired for new investments) |
| 10+ years | Gain deferred + potential exclusion | Exclusion on OZ investment's own appreciation |
