# FARTHER ONE-PAGER DESIGN BRIEF — THE BEHAVIOR GAP

## Metadata
- **Category label**: BEHAVIORAL FINANCE
- **Headline (page title)**: The Gap Between Fund and Investor
- **Subhead**: What the average equity fund investor actually earned versus what the S&P 500 delivered — measured annually since 1985 by DALBAR's Quantitative Analysis of Investor Behavior, and shown here year by year for 2021–2025 plus long-term annualized periods.

## Headline Stat Boxes
1. **30-YEAR INVESTOR RETURN** — **6.81%** — Annualized return of the Average Equity Fund Investor for the 30 years ended 12/31/2022, versus 9.65% for the S&P 500. (DALBAR, 29th Annual QAIB, 2023 edition, Executive Summary table — https://atlasfinancialinc.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/DALBAR-2023-QAIB.pdf)
2. **30-YEAR GAP** — **2.84 points a year** — The annualized shortfall over that same 30-year period, driven by buying and selling decisions rather than by fund performance. (Calculated from DALBAR 2023 QAIB figures: 9.65% − 6.81% — https://atlasfinancialinc.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/DALBAR-2023-QAIB.pdf)
3. **WORST RECENT YEAR** — **848 basis points** — The 2024 gap: the Average Equity Investor earned 16.54% while the S&P 500 returned 25.02% — the second-largest annual gap of the past decade. (DALBAR 2025 QAIB press release, 3/31/2025 — https://www.dalbar.com/PressReleases/doc/2025QAIBPressRelease.pdf)
4. **BEST RECENT YEAR** — **72 basis points** — The 2025 gap: investors earned 17.16% against the S&P 500's 17.88% — the third-smallest gap since 1985 and the narrowest since 2012. (DALBAR 2026 QAIB press release — https://www.dalbar.com/press-release/dalbars-2026-qaib-report-shows-narrower-investor-gap-amid-a-complex-and-volatile-market-year/)

Additional behavior data points worth a small sidebar:
- Average equity fund investor holding period in 2022: **4.25 years**, down from 4.36 years in 2021. (DALBAR 2023 QAIB — https://atlasfinancialinc.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/DALBAR-2023-QAIB.pdf)
- Equity withdrawals in 2025 reached **6.91% of assets**, including a record monthly withdrawal rate of **2.30% in July 2025**. (DALBAR 2026 QAIB press release — https://www.dalbar.com/press-release/dalbars-2026-qaib-report-shows-narrower-investor-gap-amid-a-complex-and-volatile-market-year/)
- Fixed income tells the same story in reverse: in 2025 the Average Fixed Income Investor earned **2.41%** against the Bloomberg Aggregate's **7.30%** — a **4.89-point** gap. (DALBAR 2026 QAIB press release — same URL)

## Insight Callouts

**INSIGHT 01 — THE FUND ISN'T THE PROBLEM**
DALBAR measures what investors actually earned, not what their funds returned. Same funds, different results. The difference is when money went in and when it came out. In 2024 that timing cost the average equity investor 8.48 points against the S&P 500.

**INSIGHT 02 — THE COST COMPOUNDS QUIETLY**
Losing 2.84 points a year sounds survivable. Over thirty years it is not. It is the difference between the market's 9.65% and an investor's 6.81% — and that spread widens every year you leave it alone. Discipline is not a personality trait. It is a process you can build.
