## Metadata
- **Category label**: PORTFOLIO PERSPECTIVE
- **Headline (page title)**: What Moves Together, What Doesn't
- **Subhead**: Historical correlation coefficients between eight major asset classes, measured on monthly returns from January 2008 through June 2025 — the arithmetic behind why a mix of assets is steadier than any one of them.

1. **GOLD vs. U.S. STOCKS** — **0.07** — Effectively no relationship over 17½ years; gold's path has been almost independent of the S&P 500. (Portfolio Visualizer, Asset Class Correlations, 01/01/2008–06/30/2025 — https://www.portfoliovisualizer.com/asset-class-correlations)

4. **DIVERSIFICATION'S 2025 PAYOFF** — **5 percentage points** — Morningstar's broadly diversified test portfolio returned 18.3% in 2025 versus 13.3% for a plain 60/40 — the widest margin since 2009, driven by falling correlations. (Morningstar, 2026 Diversification Landscape — https://www.morningstar.com/business/insights/blog/portfolio-diversification-strategy)

Supporting recent evidence that correlations move:
- Over the three-year period ended 2025, the Morningstar Global Markets ex-US Index had a correlation of **0.74** with U.S. stocks, down from as high as **0.93** in previous periods. (Morningstar — https://www.morningstar.com/portfolios/why-portfolio-diversification-has-paid-offbut-more-isnt-always-better)
- Stock/bond correlation was elevated and positive from 2022 through 2024, then returned to negative territory in 2025. (Morningstar — https://www.morningstar.com/business/insights/blog/portfolio-diversification-strategy)
- Gold was the only major asset class with a negative correlation to U.S. stocks over the trailing three years — and it rose about 70% in 2025 while remaining more volatile than U.S. stocks. (Morningstar — https://www.morningstar.com/portfolios/why-portfolio-diversification-has-paid-offbut-more-isnt-always-better)
- By year-end 2025, the three-year correlation between the Bloomberg Commodity Index and the Morningstar US Market Index fell to **0.05**. (Morningstar — https://www.morningstar.com/business/insights/blog/portfolio-diversification-strategy)

## Insight Callouts

**INSIGHT 01 — OWNING MORE ISN'T OWNING DIFFERENT**
International developed stocks correlate 0.87 with U.S. stocks. Emerging markets, 0.76. REITs, 0.75. Adding funds that all move together adds line items, not protection. Diversification comes from owning things that respond to different forces.

**INSIGHT 02 — CORRELATIONS ARE NOT FIXED**
Stocks and bonds moved together through 2022–2024, then pulled apart again in 2025. That is why a portfolio is a standing decision, not a one-time one. We rebalance because the relationships between assets keep changing, and the math only works if you keep tending it.
