## Insight Callouts

INSIGHT 01 — FEELINGS PEAK AT THE WRONG TIME
Confidence is highest when prices are highest. In March 2009, a record 70.3% of individual investors said stocks would keep falling — and the market bottomed four days later, then gained 68.6% over the next year. The emotion that feels most justified is usually the one that costs the most.

INSIGHT 02 — THE CYCLE ALWAYS COMPLETES
It took about five and a half years for the S&P 500 to climb from its 2007 record back above it in March 2013. In 2020 the same round trip took six months. You cannot know the length in advance, which is exactly why a plan beats a forecast.
