# Qualified Small Business Stock (QSBS)

**CLIENT EDUCATION GUIDE**

## Understanding Section 1202 eligibility, holding periods, and exclusion potential

Qualified Small Business Stock (QSBS) can offer a significant federal capital gains exclusion on the sale of eligible startup or small business stock, but eligibility depends on specific requirements around the company, the stock, and the holding period.

---

### Headline Numbers

**5 yrs**
Minimum holding period required from original issuance to qualify

---

**$10M+**
Potential federal exclusion per issuer, or 10x basis if greater

---

**$50M**
Typical gross asset ceiling for the issuing corporation at the time of stock issuance

## The Core Idea

Stock acquired directly from an eligible small C-corporation, held for more than five years, may qualify for exclusion of a substantial portion of the gain from federal capital gains tax upon sale, subject to per-issuer limits and specific requirements that must be verified well before a sale.

## How It Works

**STEP 1.** Confirm the issuing company meets QSBS eligibility requirements at the time the stock was issued.

**STEP 2.** Hold the stock for more than five years from the original issuance date.

**STEP 3.** At sale, calculate the eligible exclusion amount, subject to per-issuer limits.

**STEP 4.** Report the exclusion properly on the applicable tax forms.

## QSBS Exclusion vs. Standard Capital Gain

A founder sells qualifying QSBS stock with a $1,000,000 gain after a 6-year holding period.

| Scenario | Federal Capital Gains Tax Owed |
|---|---|
| Standard long-term capital gain (no QSBS) | ~$200,000 |
| Qualifying QSBS gain (full exclusion applies) | $0 |


*Hypothetical illustration only. Assumes the gain is fully within per-issuer exclusion limits and all eligibility requirements are met and verified.*

## Who This Is Designed For

- Founders and early employees holding stock in an eligible startup or small business
- Investors who directly acquired stock from an eligible small C-corporation
- Anyone approaching the five-year holding period on potentially qualifying stock

## Key Benefits at a Glance

- Can exclude a substantial portion of the gain from federal capital gains tax
- Rewards early, long-term investment in qualifying small businesses
- Exclusion applies per issuer, potentially across multiple qualifying investments
- Can be paired with gifting strategies to multiply exclusion across family members

## Important Considerations

**Assumed eligibility.** Assuming stock qualifies without verifying the issuing company met all requirements at issuance.

**Early sale.** Selling before the five-year holding period is complete, forfeiting the exclusion entirely.

**Poor documentation.** Failing to properly document QSBS eligibility, which can be difficult to reconstruct years later.
