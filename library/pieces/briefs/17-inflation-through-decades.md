## Insight Callouts

**INSIGHT 01 — CASH IS NOT SAFE, IT'S SLOW**
Cash never shows a loss on a statement. That is exactly what makes it deceptive. Ten thousand dollars parked since 2000 buys $5,349 worth of goods today. The dollars are all still there. Almost half the value is not.

**INSIGHT 02 — 2% AND 7% ARE DIFFERENT WORLDS**
The 2010s averaged 1.8% a year, and a whole generation learned to ignore inflation. The 1970s averaged 7.1% and prices nearly doubled in ten years. A plan that only works in the calm decade is not really a plan.
