## Headline Stat Boxes (4 boxes, top of page)

1. **RUSSELL'S 2026 ESTIMATE** — **4.92%** — Russell Investments' 13th annual Value of an Advisor study estimates the potential value of an advisor in the U.S. at 4.92%, or 492 basis points. (Russell Investments, *Value of an Advisor Study*, 2026, 13th edition, https://russellinvestments.com/content/dam/ri/files/us/en/financial-professional/insights/value-of-an-advisor-study.pdf)

2. **BIGGEST SINGLE SOURCE** — **2.30%** — Behavioral coaching. Russell measures it as the annualized cost borne by retail investors who chase the market, calculated as the gap between the S&P 500 Index return and actual investor returns. (Russell Investments, *Value of an Advisor Study*, 2026, https://russellinvestments.com/content/dam/ri/files/us/en/financial-professional/insights/value-of-an-advisor-study.pdf)

3. **VANGUARD'S FRAMEWORK** — **About 3%** — Vanguard's Advisor's Alpha research concludes that advisors following wealth management best practices can add up to, or even exceed, 3 percentage points in net returns. Vanguard is explicit that this is not an every-year 3%; it arrives irregularly, often during market stress. (Vanguard, *Putting a value on your value: Quantifying Vanguard Advisor's Alpha*, 2022, https://www.vanguardsouthamerica.com/content/dam/intl/americas/documents/latam/en/2022/08/mx-sa-2335954-putting-a-value-on-your-value-quantifying-vanguard-advisors-alpha.pdf)

4. **ENVESTNET'S ESTIMATE** — **~3%** — Envestnet's Capital Sigma research puts total advisor-created value at around 3% annually, with tax management alone worth about 100 basis points. (Envestnet | PMC, *Capital Sigma: The Advisor Advantage*, 2019, https://www.envestnet.com/sites/default/files/documents/PMC-CAP-SIGMA.pdf)

**Vanguard Advisor's Alpha — module-by-module potential value (2025 edition of the framework):**

| Vanguard module | Potential value added |
|---|---|
| Suitable asset allocation using broadly diversified funds/ETFs | Greater than 0 bps (significant, but too specific to each investor to quantify) |
| Investment selection | 0–100 bps |

Vanguard's own caution, in its words: this 3% "should not be expected annually" — the value-add is "likely to be very irregular," arriving in bursts during periods of market duress or euphoria. Vanguard also notes the module values are not summed, because the strategies interact.

## Insight Callouts

**INSIGHT 01 — BEHAVIOR IS THE BIGGEST LEVER**
Russell puts behavioral coaching at 2.30% of its 4.92% estimate — nearly half the total value of advice. It is not stock picking. It is the phone call that keeps you invested in March 2020 instead of selling at the bottom.

**INSIGHT 02 — THE VALUE ARRIVES IN BURSTS**
Vanguard is careful to say the 3% does not show up evenly each year. It concentrates in the moments that matter most: a market panic, a windfall, a retirement date, a tax decision. That is exactly when guidance is hardest to replace.
