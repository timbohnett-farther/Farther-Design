## The Core Idea

Beyond the standard pre-tax or Roth 401(k) contribution limit, some plans allow additional after-tax contributions up to the overall plan limit. If the plan permits in-plan conversions or in-service withdrawals, those after-tax contributions can be converted to Roth relatively quickly, minimizing taxable growth before conversion.

## How It Works

**STEP 1.** Confirm the employer plan allows after-tax contributions beyond the standard deferral limit.

**STEP 2.** Confirm the plan also allows in-plan Roth conversions or in-service withdrawals.

**STEP 3.** Contribute after-tax dollars up to the overall plan limit, after accounting for other contributions and match.

**STEP 4.** Convert those after-tax contributions to Roth promptly, minimizing taxable growth before conversion.

## Who This Is Designed For

- High-income earners who have maxed out standard pre-tax or Roth 401(k) contributions
- Employees whose plan specifically allows after-tax contributions and in-plan conversions
- Households wanting significantly more Roth savings capacity than standard limits allow

## Key Benefits at a Glance

- Dramatically expands annual Roth savings capacity beyond standard limits
- Minimizes taxable growth through prompt in-plan conversion
- Builds substantial tax-free growth for retirement
- Complements, rather than replaces, standard pre-tax or Roth contributions

## Important Considerations

**Assumed availability.** Assuming this strategy is available without first confirming the specific plan features required.

**Delayed conversion.** Delaying the conversion of after-tax contributions, allowing taxable growth to accumulate before conversion.

**Miscalculated room.** Miscalculating available after-tax contribution room after accounting for employer match and other contributions.
