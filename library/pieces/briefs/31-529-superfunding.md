# 529 Superfunding

**CLIENT EDUCATION GUIDE**

## Front-loading up to five years of annual exclusions into one education account

529 superfunding allows a contributor to front-load up to five years' worth of annual gift tax exclusions into a single 529 plan contribution, jump-starting tax-advantaged growth for education savings well beyond what one year's exclusion would allow.

**$95,000**

---

**$190,000**
Combined superfunding limit for a married couple contributing together

---

**5 yrs**
Number of years the gift is spread across for gift tax reporting purposes

## The Core Idea

Normally, gifting more than the annual exclusion to one recipient uses lifetime exemption or requires gift tax. A special election for 529 contributions allows a single lump sum, up to five years' worth of exclusions, to be treated as if made ratably over five years, avoiding any use of lifetime exemption as long as no other gifts are made to that beneficiary during the period.

## How It Works

**STEP 1.** Contribute up to five times the annual exclusion amount to a 529 plan in a single year.

**STEP 2.** File the required gift tax return election to spread the gift over five years.

**STEP 3.** Avoid making additional gifts to the same beneficiary during the five-year averaging period.

**STEP 4.** The full contribution begins growing tax-advantaged immediately, well ahead of a standard annual funding approach.

## Superfunding vs. Annual Contributions

A grandparent compares contributing $19,000 annually to a 529 versus superfunding $95,000 immediately, both growing at 6% over 18 years.

| Approach | Total Contributed | Illustrative Value at Year 18 |
|---|---|---|
| Annual $19,000 contributions | $342,000 (18 yrs) | ~$570,000 |
| Superfunded $95,000 lump sum, Year 1 | $95,000 | ~$271,000, plus any later gifts |

## Who This Is Designed For

- Grandparents or parents wanting to jump-start education savings significantly
- Anyone with a lump sum available for gifting toward a beneficiary's education
- Households wanting to maximize tax-advantaged compounding time in a 529 account

## Key Benefits at a Glance

- Maximizes the time horizon for tax-advantaged growth in the account
- Avoids using any lifetime gift tax exemption if properly elected
- Provides a significant head start on education funding goals
- Can be combined across multiple grandparents or contributors for even larger amounts

## Important Considerations

**Missed election.** Failing to file the required gift tax return election to spread the gift over five years.

**Additional gifting conflict.** Making other gifts to the same beneficiary during the five-year period without accounting for the overlap.

**Premature death consideration.** Not understanding the special rules that apply if the contributor dies during the five-year averaging period.
