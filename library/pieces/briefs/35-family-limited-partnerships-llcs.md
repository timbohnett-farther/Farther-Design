# Family Limited Partnerships and LLCs

**CLIENT EDUCATION GUIDE**

## Separating control, economics, and transferability in family-owned assets

Family Limited Partnerships (FLPs) and LLCs allow a family to hold assets like real estate or business interests in a single entity, separating control, held by a general partner or manager, from economic ownership, held by limited partners or members, often facilitating gifting at a discounted value.

## The Core Idea

By structuring family assets inside an FLP or LLC, senior family members can retain management control as the general partner or manager while gifting limited partnership or membership interests to the next generation. Because those gifted interests lack control and are not easily marketable, they may be valued at a meaningful discount for gift tax purposes, allowing more value to transfer using the same amount of gift tax exemption.

## How It Works

**STEP 1.** Contribute family assets, such as real estate or business interests, into a newly formed FLP or LLC.

**STEP 2.** Retain a general partner or manager interest, preserving control over the entity's decisions.

**STEP 3.** Gift limited partner or non-managing member interests to the next generation, often over multiple years.

**STEP 4.** Obtain a qualified appraisal supporting any valuation discount applied to the gifted interests.

## Direct Gift vs. Discounted Entity Interest Gift

A $1,000,000 pro-rata share of family real estate is gifted directly versus as a discounted limited partnership interest.

| Approach | Gift Tax Exemption Used | Underlying Value Transferred |
|---|---|---|
| Direct pro-rata gift of real estate | $1,000,000 | $1,000,000 |
| Gift of limited partnership interest (30% discount) | $700,000 | $1,000,000 |


*Hypothetical illustration only. Valuation discounts require a qualified independent appraisal and are subject to IRS scrutiny; actual discounts vary.*

## Who This Is Designed For

- Families holding significant real estate, business interests, or investment assets together
- Households wanting to transfer wealth to younger generations while retaining control
- Anyone considering valuation discount planning as part of a broader gifting strategy

## Key Benefits at a Glance

- Allows senior family members to retain control while transferring economic value
- Can enable more value to transfer per dollar of gift tax exemption used
- Consolidates family asset management into a single, organized entity
- Facilitates structured, multi-year gifting programs to younger generations

## Important Considerations

**Aggressive discounting.** Applying discounts without a properly supported, independent qualified appraisal.

**Insufficient business purpose.** Failing to establish a legitimate business or management purpose for the entity beyond tax benefits alone.

**Retained control risk.** Retaining excessive control or benefits in a way that could cause the IRS to challenge the estate exclusion.
