# Private Equity Commitment Pacing

**CLIENT EDUCATION GUIDE**

## Managing capital calls, distributions, vintage years, and illiquidity

Building a private equity allocation isn't a single investment decision, it requires a deliberate commitment pacing plan across multiple fund vintages, since capital is called gradually over years and distributed unevenly as investments mature.

## The Core Idea

A single private equity commitment doesn't behave like a lump-sum investment, capital is called gradually, often unevenly, and distributions don't begin flowing back for several years. Committing to funds across multiple vintage years smooths this unevenness, ensuring the household isn't consistently over-committed in cash reserves or under-invested in private markets.

## How It Works

**STEP 1.** Determine an overall target allocation to private equity within the total portfolio.

**STEP 2.** Commit to new funds across multiple vintage years, rather than concentrating commitments in a single year.

**STEP 3.** Model expected capital calls and distributions across all outstanding commitments each year.

**STEP 4.** Adjust future commitment pacing based on actual call and distribution activity, maintaining the target allocation over time.

## Single-Vintage vs. Multi-Vintage Commitment Pacing

A household targeting a $10,000,000 private equity allocation compares committing it all to one fund versus pacing across 5 vintage years.

| Approach | Cash Reserve Needed for Calls | Portfolio J-Curve Smoothness |
|---|---|---|
| Single $10M commitment, one vintage | Concentrated, uneven call timing | Sharp, concentrated J-curve |
| $2M commitments across 5 vintages | Smoother, more predictable | Blended, smoother overall curve |


*Hypothetical illustration only. Actual capital call timing and distribution patterns vary significantly by fund manager and strategy.*

## Who This Is Designed For

- Qualified investors building or expanding a private equity allocation
- Households wanting to avoid concentrated liquidity strain from capital calls
- Anyone new to private markets needing a structured, multi-year commitment plan

## Key Benefits at a Glance

- Smooths the uneven cash flow pattern typical of private equity investing
- Reduces the risk of being caught without adequate liquidity for a capital call
- Diversifies exposure across market conditions present in different vintage years
- Supports a more mature, steady-state private equity program over time

## Important Considerations

**Single-vintage concentration.** Committing an entire target allocation to funds in a single vintage year.

**Cash reserve mismatch.** Failing to maintain adequate liquidity reserves for unpredictable capital calls.

**Ignoring the J-curve.** Underestimating the multi-year lag before meaningful distributions begin flowing back.
