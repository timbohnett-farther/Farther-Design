## Charitable Remainder Unitrusts for highly appreciated stock, real estate, or closely held business interests

A Charitable Remainder Unitrust (CRUT) lets an owner of a highly appreciated, concentrated asset contribute it to the trust, sell it without immediate capital gains tax, and receive an income stream based on a fixed percentage of the trust's value each year, with the remainder ultimately passing to charity.

---

### Headline Numbers

**0%**
Immediate capital gains tax owed at the trust level on the CRUT's sale of the contributed asset

---

**5%+**
Typical minimum annual unitrust payout percentage, revalued each year

---

**10%+**
Minimum actuarial remainder value generally required to pass to charity at funding

## The Core Idea

Because the unitrust payout is a percentage of the trust's value, recalculated annually, the income stream can grow if the trust's investments perform well, offering some inflation protection compared to a fixed annuity structure, while still deferring capital gains tax on the original contributed asset.

## How It Works

**STEP 1.** Contribute a highly appreciated, low-basis asset, stock, real estate, or a business interest, to the CRUT.

**STEP 2.** The trust sells the asset without immediate capital gains tax at the trust level.

**STEP 3.** The trust pays the income beneficiary a fixed percentage of its revalued balance each year.

**STEP 4.** At the end of the trust term, the remaining balance passes to the named charity.

## Outright Sale vs. CRUT-Funded Income

A $5,000,000 closely held business interest with a $500,000 basis is sold outright versus contributed to a 5% CRUT.

| Approach | Immediate Tax Due | Year 1 Income-Generating Base |
|---|---|---|
| Sell outright, reinvest after-tax | ~$1,071,000 | ~$3,929,000 |
| Contribute to CRUT, sell inside trust | $0 | $5,000,000 (full pre-tax value) |


*Hypothetical illustration only. Assumes a 23.8% combined capital gains/NIIT rate. CRUT distributions are taxable to the recipient under tiered ordering rules.*

## Who This Is Designed For

- Owners of highly appreciated, concentrated stock, real estate, or closely held business interests
- Charitably inclined individuals wanting to diversify a concentrated holding into income
- UHNW households seeking both lifetime income and a meaningful charitable legacy

## Key Benefits at a Glance

- Defers capital gains tax entirely at the trust level on the contributed asset
- Converts an illiquid or concentrated holding into a diversified income stream
- Offers a payout that can grow with strong trust investment performance
- Provides a partial charitable income tax deduction at the time of funding

## Important Considerations

**Illiquidity mismatch.** Contributing an asset the trust cannot practically sell or value annually, complicating the unitrust structure.

**Income variability surprise.** Underestimating that unitrust payments can decline in a down market, unlike a fixed annuity.

**Irrevocability oversight.** Underappreciating that the CRUT, once funded, cannot be unwound.
