# Preferred Equity Recapitalizations: Taking Some Chips Off the Table Without Selling the Business

**CLIENT EDUCATION GUIDE**

## Founder liquidity and partial diversification through a recapitalization; compares common equity, preferred equity, control, downside, and future upside

A preferred equity recapitalization allows a founder to receive partial liquidity from their business today, often while retaining significant control and future upside, by restructuring some ownership into preferred equity with a defined return preference rather than selling the business outright.

---

### Headline Numbers

**Partial**

---

**Retained**

---

**Preference-based**

## The Core Idea

Rather than selling the entire business, or continuing to hold 100% illiquid ownership, a preferred equity recapitalization creates a middle path, a portion of the founder's equity is exchanged for cash or converted into preferred equity carrying a defined return preference, while common equity ownership, and often day-to-day control, is substantially retained for continued participation in the business's future growth.

## How It Works

**STEP 1.** Evaluate the business's current valuation and the founder's desired liquidity amount.

**STEP 2.** Structure a portion of ownership as preferred equity, carrying a defined return preference senior to common equity.

**STEP 3.** The founder receives cash proceeds from the recapitalization while retaining common equity ownership and typically operational control.

**STEP 4.** Future growth and eventual sale proceeds are shared between preferred and common equity according to the negotiated structure.

## Full Sale vs. Preferred Equity Recapitalization

A founder with a $50,000,000 business compares selling the entire company versus a recapitalization providing $15,000,000 of immediate liquidity.

| Approach | Immediate Liquidity | Retained Ownership/Control | Future Upside Participation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Full sale | $50,000,000 | None | None |
| Preferred equity recap | $15,000,000 | Substantial common equity retained | Yes, on remaining common equity |


*Hypothetical illustration only. Actual liquidity amounts, retained ownership, and control depend on specific deal negotiation and business circumstances.*

## Who This Is Designed For

- Founders wanting partial liquidity and diversification without fully exiting their business
- Business owners seeking to de-risk personal net worth while continuing to grow the company
- Anyone evaluating recapitalization as a middle path between full illiquidity and a complete sale

## Key Benefits at a Glance

- Provides meaningful liquidity without requiring a full exit from the business
- Allows the founder to retain substantial ownership, and often operational control
- Diversifies a founder's personal net worth away from a single concentrated business asset
- Preserves future upside participation in the business's continued growth

## Important Considerations

**Underestimating preference terms.** Failing to fully understand how the preferred equity's return preference affects the founder's eventual proceeds in various future sale scenarios.

**Control dilution surprise.** Assuming operational control is fully preserved without carefully negotiating governance terms.

**Comparing only headline numbers.** Evaluating the recapitalization solely on immediate liquidity without modeling long-term proceeds under different future outcomes.
