## 529 Plans and Education Savings

### 🎓 What It Is
A 529 plan is a tax-advantaged investment account designed for education expenses, allowing tax-free growth and tax-free withdrawals when used for qualified education costs, including K-12 tuition, college, and certain apprenticeship programs.

### 🎓 Why It Matters
While contributions are never deductible on your federal return, more than 30 states offer a state income tax deduction or credit for contributions — but the rules, amounts, and whether you must use your own state's plan vary considerably.[1]

### 🎓 The Key Insight
Because state tax benefits differ so widely, it's worth confirming your specific state's rules before assuming a particular 529 plan is the "best" choice — the best plan for a Texas resident may not be the best plan for a New York resident.

## Benefits of 529 Plans
- Tax-free growth and tax-free qualified withdrawals at the federal level
- Potential state income tax deduction or credit, depending on your state of residence
- High contribution limits compared to other education savings vehicles
- Funds can now be used for K-12 tuition and certain apprenticeship costs, not just college
- Unused funds can be transferred to another family member or, within limits, rolled into a Roth IRA for the beneficiary

## State-Specific Considerations
State tax treatment of 529 contributions varies dramatically:
- **Nine states have no state income tax** (including Texas, Florida, and Nevada), so there's no state deduction available regardless of which plan you choose.[1]
- **States like New Mexico, South Carolina, and West Virginia** allow taxpayers to deduct the entire 529 contribution amount, with no cap.[1]
- **Four states with income taxes offer no 529 deduction at all**: California, Hawaii, Kentucky, and North Carolina.[1]
- **"Tax parity" states** (including Arizona, Kansas, Minnesota, Missouri, Montana, Ohio, Pennsylvania) allow residents to claim the state tax benefit regardless of which state's 529 plan they choose — most other states require using the in-state plan to claim the deduction.[1]

| Family Location | State 529 Tax Treatment | Illustrative Annual State Tax Benefit |
