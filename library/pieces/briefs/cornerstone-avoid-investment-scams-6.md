## How to Avoid Investment Scams

### 🚩 What They Are
Investment scams range from classic Ponzi schemes to modern cryptocurrency fraud and "pig butchering" schemes, often using pressure tactics, promises of guaranteed returns, or fabricated urgency to convince victims to hand over money.

### 🚩 Why It Matters
Investment fraud costs Americans billions of dollars annually, and increasingly sophisticated tactics — including AI-generated voices and deepfakes impersonating trusted contacts — make scams harder to spot than ever.

### 🚩 The Key Insight
Nearly every legitimate investment carries some risk and takes time to grow — any offer promising "guaranteed" high returns with little or no risk should be treated as an immediate red flag.

## Warning Signs to Watch For
- Promises of guaranteed or unusually high returns with little or no risk
- Pressure to invest quickly, before you have time to research or consult an advisor
- Requests for payment via wire transfer, cryptocurrency, or gift cards
- Unregistered investments or unlicensed individuals soliciting your money
- Vague or overly complex explanations of how the investment actually generates returns

## Benefits of Staying Vigilant
- Protects your life savings from irreversible fraud losses
- Encourages verifying credentials before working with any financial professional
- Supports better decision-making by slowing down high-pressure situations
- Reduces vulnerability to increasingly sophisticated AI-driven scams targeting older investors
- Reinforces the value of working with a known, licensed, and verifiable advisor
