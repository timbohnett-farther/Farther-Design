## Why Chasing Past Performance Backfires

### 🚫 What It Means
"Chasing performance" means buying an investment because it recently performed well, assuming the trend will continue — often ignoring whether it still fits your overall strategy.

### 🚫 Why It Matters
Markets and sectors rotate. The best-performing asset class or fund in one year is frequently among the worst performers in the following years, as valuations become stretched and conditions shift.

### 🚫 The Key Insight
Past performance is one of the weakest predictors of future returns — a lesson so consistent that every fund prospectus is legally required to state it.

## Benefits of Avoiding the Performance-Chasing Trap
- Reduces the risk of buying near a peak, right before a reversal
- Keeps your portfolio aligned with your actual strategy rather than trending headlines
- Protects against emotional, FOMO-driven decisions
- Encourages evaluating investments on fundamentals and fit, not just recent returns
- Supports a more consistent, disciplined long-term approach
