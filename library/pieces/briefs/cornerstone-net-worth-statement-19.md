## Understanding Your Net Worth Statement

### 📊 What It Is
A net worth statement is a simple summary listing everything you own (assets) minus everything you owe (liabilities), resulting in your total net worth — a foundational snapshot of your overall financial position.

### 📊 Why It Matters
Unlike checking a single account balance, a net worth statement gives you the complete picture across every account, property, and debt — the real starting point for any meaningful financial plan.

### 📊 The Key Insight
Net worth isn't just a number to track for its own sake — it's a tool for measuring progress toward your goals over time and for spotting risks (like too much debt relative to assets) that might not be obvious otherwise.

## Benefits of Tracking Your Net Worth
- Provides a clear, complete view of your financial position at a single point in time
- Establishes a baseline for measuring progress year over year
- Reveals if debt is growing faster than assets, an early warning sign worth addressing
- Supports more informed conversations with your advisor about goals and priorities
- Helps identify assets that may be overlooked or underutilized (old accounts, unused property)

## What to Include
- **Assets:** cash, investment accounts, retirement accounts, real estate, business interests, and other valuable property
- **Liabilities:** mortgages, loans, credit card balances, and any other outstanding debts
- **Net Worth:** total assets minus total liabilities
