## Risk Tolerance vs. Risk Capacity

### 🎯 What They Are
Risk tolerance is your emotional comfort with investment volatility — how well you sleep at night when markets drop. Risk capacity is your financial ability to withstand losses without jeopardizing your goals, based on factors like time horizon, income stability, and cash reserves.

### 🎯 Why It Matters
These two factors don't always match. Someone may feel emotionally comfortable taking on significant risk but lack the financial capacity to recover from a large loss, or vice versa — feel anxious about risk despite having decades to recover.

### 🎯 The Key Insight
A well-built portfolio balances both dimensions, rather than defaulting to just one. Ignoring either one can lead to a portfolio that's mismatched to your actual situation.

## Benefits of Understanding Both Dimensions
- Prevents taking on more risk than your financial situation can actually support
- Avoids being too conservative relative to your true capacity, which can hurt long-term growth
- Creates a more honest, personalized starting point for portfolio construction
- Reduces the likelihood of panic-selling during downturns because expectations are realistic
- Supports better conversations between you and your advisor about tradeoffs

## Seeing It in Action

> 💡 **KEY STAT CALLOUT:** High risk tolerance doesn't always mean high risk capacity


Consider two people, both self-described as "comfortable with risk" (high risk tolerance).

| Person | Risk Tolerance | Risk Capacity | Appropriate Approach |
|---|---|---|---|
| Person A | High — comfortable with volatility | High — 30 years to retirement, stable income | Growth-oriented portfolio aligns well with both factors |
| Person B | High — comfortable with volatility | Low — retiring next year, relies on portfolio for income | Despite comfort with risk, capacity constraints call for a more conservative approach |

Person B's stated comfort with risk doesn't change the fact that a major loss right before retirement could be financially damaging.
