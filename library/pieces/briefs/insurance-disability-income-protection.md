# Intelligent Wealth Series: Disability Insurance — The Coverage Most People Skip

## Protect the Income Before You Protect the Estate

**For professionals, executives, and business owners whose ability to earn is their largest economic asset.**

Many households insure a house, a car, and jewelry before they insure the income that funds all of them. For most working-age people, the present value of future earnings is one of the largest assets on the balance sheet. Disability coverage exists to protect that earning power if health interrupts the plan.

## What the Policy Is Really Insuring

### Income, not inconvenience

Disability insurance is designed to replace a portion of income if illness or injury prevents work. The main planning question is not whether disability is possible. It is how long the household could function if earned income stopped for two years, five years, or permanently.

### Group versus individual coverage

Employer coverage may provide some protection, but it often replaces only part of income and may not be portable. Individual policies can offer stronger contractual definitions, supplemental benefit levels, and portability when jobs change.

### The planning point

The household may feel insured because a benefit exists. The real question is whether that benefit matches the lifestyle, debt service, savings targets, and dependent needs already in motion.
