# Intelligent Wealth Series: Qualified Longevity Annuity Contracts (QLACs)

## A Specific Tool for a Specific Retirement Risk

**For retirement households evaluating whether carving out part of an IRA for delayed lifetime income can reduce future distribution pressure and hedge late-life longevity risk.**

A QLAC is not a broad annuity category. It is a highly specific retirement planning tool with defined tax rules. Its job is narrow but meaningful: use part of qualified retirement assets to purchase income that begins later in life, potentially reducing required minimum distribution pressure on the excluded amount before income begins.

## Why It Exists

### Longevity insurance

A QLAC creates a future income stream that begins at an advanced age, often 80 or 85 depending on the contract terms. It is designed to insure against the risk of living longer than expected and depleting other retirement resources.

### RMD coordination

Because eligible dollars used to purchase the QLAC can reduce the IRA balance counted for RMD purposes up to the applicable rules and limits, some retirees use it to reshape the timing of taxable retirement income.

## What the Numbers Look Like in Practice

### Illustrative impact

| Measure | Without QLAC | With $200,000 QLAC |
|---|---|---|
| IRA balance used for RMD math | $2,000,000 | $1,800,000 |
| Approximate first-year RMD at 73 | $75,500 | $67,900 |
| Reduction in first-year RMD | — | $7,600 |
| Future late-life income floor | None from QLAC | Begins at contract payout age |

### What it does not do

A QLAC does not make taxes disappear or solve a weak retirement plan. It changes the pattern of income and may improve resilience late in life if that specific problem matters to the household.
