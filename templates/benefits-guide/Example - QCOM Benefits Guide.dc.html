Retirement & Savings
  • Tiered 401(k) match — 100% of first $1,500, 50% of next $1,500, 33% of next $7,500, 10% thereafter; ~$7,150 at the IRS max.
  • Match vests 50% at 1 yr, 100% at 2 yrs — flag for anyone weighing a move.
  • Pre-tax + Roth · in-service distributions at 59½ · Fidelity BrokerageLink window.
  • No pension / DB plan — retirement adequacy rides entirely on the 401(k) + NQDCP.
Mega backdoor Roth — available
After-tax contributions with in-plan conversion. Confirm after-tax is on and conversions are automatic or frequent.
Equity & Stock Plans
  • RSUs vest annually over 3 years in equal installments — broad-based and executive grants alike.
  • PSUs (executives) — 3-yr cliff on relative TSR + Adjusted EPS; capped 2x target; no above-target payout if absolute TSR is negative.
  • ~69M shares available under the 2023 LTIP; 24M-share increase on the Mar 17, 2026 ballot (Proposal 5).
  • Buybacks offset dilution — $15.0B program (Nov 2024), $7.2B remaining at FY-end.
ESPP — 15% discount, look-back
85% of the lesser of offering-start or purchase-date price. Semi-annual offerings (Feb 1–Jul 31, Aug 1–Jan 31); up to 15% of base pay, IRS $25K cap. Disqualifying dispositions taxed as ordinary income.
Cash & Bonus
  • Annual Cash Incentive Plan (ACIP) on one-year Adjusted Revenues + Adjusted Operating Income, with a human-capital modifier.
  • Payouts capped at 2x target; paid following fiscal year-end (last Sunday in September).
  • ACIP awards are deferrable into the NQDCP for eligible senior employees.
Deferred Comp (NQDCP)
  • Defer base salary + ACIP beyond 401(k) limits; company match = 25% of deferrals up to 4% of base, plus an ACIP-deferral component.
  • Distribution elections set at initial deferral (§409A) — timing is largely locked once made.
  • Unfunded, general-asset plan — balances are unsecured obligations; size deferrals with employer-credit risk in mind.
Health, Family & Lifestyle
  • "Live+Well Work+Well" — premium-free health option; PPO/HDHP/HMO at larger sites; on-site health centers at major campuses.
  • 100% company-paid dental · ~$750/yr employer HSA seed · life ~3x base · AD&D + disability.
  • Unlimited PTO (many roles) · 12 wks paid maternity/paternity · adoption + fertility · EAP.
  • Tuition to $10,500 (graduate) · charitable gift match to $10,000/yr.
Executive Programs — what they get
  • Performance-weighted pay — 61% of CEO / 60% of NEO target pay performance-based; ≥90% of NEO target comp variable.
  • CEO Amon FY25 SCT total $29.7M; ACIP + PSU payouts both capped at 2x.
  • NQDCP for comp above qualified caps (25% match on deferrals to 4% of base).
  • Ownership guidelines — 10x base (CEO), 2x (other officers); all NEOs compliant at Dec 15, 2025. No hedging, no pledging.
By design — what Qualcomm omits
  • No employment contracts — all executive officers are at-will.
  • No excise-tax gross-ups and no single-trigger CIC severance; equity acceleration is double-trigger only.
  • No pension or SERP — for executives or the broad workforce.
  • Advisor angle: the NQDCP is the only supplemental vehicle — election windows and unsecured-creditor risk do the planning work a SERP would.
!
Concentration & Dilution — two exposures to manage
Overlapping RSU grants + look-back ESPP purchases + any QCOM in BrokerageLink stack into single-ticker concentration — set a written, rules-based diversification policy. Separately, 9.3% FY25 dilution plus the requested 24M-share LTIP increase is elevated versus the 1–3% typical of large-cap peers; buyback pace ($7.2B remaining) is the offset to watch, especially amid M&A like the 2025 Alphawave acquisition.
★ Advisor Talking Points — the meeting agenda
  1. Maximize the ESPP, then sell systematically. 15% + look-back is a strong low-risk return; mind §423 disposition rules.
  2. Turn on the Mega Backdoor Roth. After-tax + in-plan conversion is available — confirm it's switched on.
  3. Mind the match vesting cliff. 100% at 2 years — factor into any transition timing; the tiered formula front-loads value.
  4. Put a rule around concentration. Sell-at-vest default or a hard QCOM ceiling across RSUs, ESPP, and BrokerageLink.
  5. Size NQDCP deferrals deliberately. Unsecured general obligation — weigh tax deferral against employer-credit risk; elections lock at deferral.
  6. Plan around the lean executive structure. No pension, no SERP, no severance contracts — equity + NQDCP carry transition planning.
  7. Watch the March 17 vote. The 24M-share LTIP increase and say-on-pay signal how dilution pressure evolves.
