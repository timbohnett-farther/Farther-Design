Client Decision Deck
Helps an advisor lead one consequential decision, not deliver a lecture. Built for one household, with their data. Not part of the education series; for group sessions use the Group Education Deck. The example decision is a concentrated stock position. Primary message: every financial decision affects another, and Farther helps ensure those decisions work together.
Structure. Open with the client's question (02), make complexity visible (03–04), quantify (05, 08), show tradeoffs honestly (06–07, 09), sequence (10), end with a decision agenda (11). Farther stays present but restrained.
Layout. 16:9, one idea per slide, three visual zones maximum, 45–60 visible words, 24px minimum type, sentence-case headlines under 12 words. Nuance goes in speaker notes.
Components. Client question banner, total-wealth connection bar (active domains filled navy), decision card, strategy comparison card, coordination map, scenario label chip on every projection.
Data. Scenario labels: client-specific analysis, illustrative scenario, historical analysis, planning estimate, third-party estimate. Charts from assets/charts/farther-charts.js. No 3D, gauges, busy pies or red/green-only encoding.
Personalization. {​{Double-brace}​} fields for client and advisor data. Advisor fields fill from data/advisors.json. Missing client data: drop the field or go qualitative. Never invent client data.
Deliverables. Client deck, presenter version with speaker notes, PDF export, editable source, one-page meeting takeaway (T1). Condensed disclosure on the final slide.
