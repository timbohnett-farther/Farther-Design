Wireframes · US Letter · three layers
Client Review Package
A quarterly review of the client's whole financial life, not only their investments. The client executive report (pages 01–10) is built for the meeting and the portal. The appendix (A1–A3) holds source detail. The advisor meeting brief (B1–B2) is internal only. Sample figures are hypothetical.
Build rules. Pages stack in a single column, one per row; don't use canvas mode. Printable 8.5 × 11. Plain-English headline on every page, stating the finding. As-of date on every section header. Status chips (On track, Needs attention, Decision required, In progress) always carry an explanation, a dollar implication and a next action; never a bare traffic light. Advisor slot on the cover only. Farther diagram icons in dashboard tiles and service lists.
Data safeguards. Source hierarchy: custodian for balances and returns, tax return for prior-year facts, financial plan for goals and assumptions, CRM for context, email only for commitments and life events. Flag stale or mixed-period data. Tag claims Verified, Inferred, Pending or Recommendation. Advisor approves every recommendation and every tax, estate and performance statement before release.
