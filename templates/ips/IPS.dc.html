Investment Policy Statement
A decision framework, not an investment manual. A client can read it in five minutes, and an auditor can trace every statement back to documented client facts. Page 1 is the Client Investment Mandate, page 2 is Portfolio Guidelines and Governance, and the Advisor QA page stays internal. Sample household and figures are hypothetical.
Include. Scope and authority, a plain-English mandate, ranked objectives, a success definition (goal funding, not a return target), horizon and cash flows, a liquidity reserve, risk willingness and capacity, the allocation with ranges, implementation rules, restrictions, rebalancing and review triggers, roles, signatures, and the condensed disclosure.
Leave out. Capital-market commentary, fund or security lists, modern portfolio theory explainers, generic risk boilerplate already in the ADV, manager due diligence, and any return promise. Those belong in proposals, fact sheets and review materials.
