What we noticed, and what to discuss
Planning observations from your return. Please review them with your tax professional before you act.
01
IRA withdrawals and gains are linked
Each extra $1,000 from the IRA costs about $280 in tax, because it pushes gains out of the 0% band. Timing withdrawals and gain sales together could lower the combined cost.
Advisor
02
Roth conversions: weigh about 28% now against later
There's $12,908 of room in the 12% bracket and $55,560 before IRMAA. Because of the gains effect, converting would cost about 28% on the margin. Compare that with your expected rate once required distributions begin.
Advisor
03
Charitable gifts gave no deduction
Your $4,500 in gifts fell inside the standard deduction. Grouping two or more years of gifts, possibly through a donor-advised fund, could push itemized deductions above $33,100. Qualified charitable distributions from an IRA become available at age 70½.
You + CPA
04
Some of the senior deduction is phasing out
MAGI above $150,000 cut Daniel's $6,000 senior deduction by $602. Each $1,000 less MAGI restores $60. When Priya turns 65, she adds her own deduction, subject to the same phase-out.
CPA
05
A $1,766 refund means you overpaid during the year
Your AGI was over $150,000, so the 2026 safe harbor is 110% of this year's tax, or $14,667. Withholding from IRA distributions counts as paid evenly through the year, which can help with timing.
CPA
06
Check where your interest-paying holdings sit
$6,200 of taxable interest was taxed at 12%. Holding bonds in the IRA, or keeping munis only where their after-tax yield wins, may lower drag on this income.
