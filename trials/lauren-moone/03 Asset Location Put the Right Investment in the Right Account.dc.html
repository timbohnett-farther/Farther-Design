Tax-aware investing
Matching bonds, equities, REITs, and tax-efficient assets to account types
The same overall allocation can produce meaningfully different after-tax returns depending purely on which account holds which asset, without changing a single underlying investment.
How it works
Bonds and REITs generate ordinary income annually and are best sheltered in tax-deferred accounts. Broad index funds and municipal bonds are already tax-efficient and fit well in taxable accounts. Getting this placement right, across every account a household holds, can add measurable after-tax return every year.
Step 1
Identify the tax character of each holding, ordinary income, qualified dividends, or tax-free.
Step 2
Place high-tax-cost assets in tax-deferred accounts.
Step 3
Place tax-efficient assets in taxable accounts.
Step 4
Maintain the same total allocation across accounts, only the location changes.
The Location Alpha Effect
On a $2,000,000 portfolio growing at 7% gross, a 0.5% annual after-tax improvement from better asset location compounds meaningfully.
