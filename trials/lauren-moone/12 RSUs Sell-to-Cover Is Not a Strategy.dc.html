Managing concentrated employer exposure after vesting
Sell-to-cover, where just enough shares are sold at vesting to cover the tax withholding, is a default mechanism, not a deliberate strategy, and relying on it alone often leaves employees increasingly concentrated in employer stock over time.
How it works
Each RSU vesting event is an opportunity to make an active decision about how much employer stock to hold versus sell, based on the household's total concentration, tax situation, and diversification goals, rather than defaulting to the minimum withholding sale.
Step 1
RSUs vest and are taxed as ordinary income at that day's fair market value.
Step 2
Sell-to-cover automatically sells enough shares to satisfy tax withholding.
Step 3
Absent a deliberate decision, the remaining shares are simply held, growing the concentrated position.
Step 4
A deliberate plan instead sets a target sale percentage at each vest, based on total concentration.

RSUs: Sell-to-Cover Is Not a Strategy · fit and reference
Who this is designed for
Employees receiving regular RSU vesting from their employer
Anyone whose employer stock concentration has grown without an active decision
Households wanting a systematic plan rather than a default withholding mechanism
Key benefits at a glance
Establishes a deliberate, repeatable diversification process
Reduces single-stock concentration risk over time
Keeps tax withholding and diversification decisions separate
Can be coordinated with blackout periods and trading policies
Important considerations
False sense of diversification
Treating sell-to-cover as sufficient diversification, when it typically only covers taxes, not concentration risk.
No target
Failing to set a target allocation to employer stock and rebalance toward it at each vest.
Compliance gaps
Ignoring blackout periods or insider trading policies when planning sales beyond sell-to-cover.
Quick reference
Default mechanism
Sell-to-cover (tax withholding only)
Tax treatment at vest
Ordinary income on FMV
Tax treatment after vest
Capital gains on subsequent moves
Risk if unmanaged
Growing concentration each vest
Best practice
Deliberate sale target at each vest
