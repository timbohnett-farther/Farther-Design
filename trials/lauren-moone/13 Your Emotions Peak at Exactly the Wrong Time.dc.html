The curve is an illustrative behavioral framework (Westcore Funds, 1998; Russell Investments), not a forecast. Assigning dates to stages is interpretive. The 2020 cycle ran the same loop in under seven months: peak Feb 19, 2020 (3,386.15), low Mar 23 (2,237.40, -33.9%), new record Aug 18, 2020.
Why this matters
Confidence is highest when prices are highest. In March 2009, a record 70.3% of individual investors said stocks would keep falling — and the market bottomed four days later, then gained 68.6% over the next year. The emotion that feels most justified is usually the one that costs the most.
