Why this matters
Investing the whole amount right away beat easing it in about two-thirds of the time in every market Vanguard measured, from the U.S. to emerging markets. The reason is simple: markets rise more often than they fall, so cash held back gives up returns. The longer you stretch the entry, the wider that gap gets.
