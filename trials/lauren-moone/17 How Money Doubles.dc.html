How money doubles: the rule of 72
Divide 72 by your annual return and you get the approximate number of years it takes money to double — a shortcut that shows how much of long-term wealth is created by time, not timing.
The shortcut
72 ÷ return
= years to double an investment

Why this matters
At 6% a year, money takes about 12 years to double. At 12%, it takes about 6. Two doublings instead of one, over the same stretch of life, is the whole argument for owning growth assets and paying attention to costs.
