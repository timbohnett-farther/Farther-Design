Voice, and the things that get flagged
Write it this way
Second person, sentence case, no terminal period on a single-sentence headline
En dashes for ranges without spaces, em dashes with spaces, Oxford commas always
Convey what the source says. Do not editorialize, and never rewrite copy a user supplies verbatim.
Candid without being combative. Cite the number rather than characterizing it.
Never ships
Emoji, accent colors outside the palette, or a call to action closing the piece
Advisor names, emails, phone numbers, or a "prepared for" block
A figure without a source or a hypothetical-illustration note
