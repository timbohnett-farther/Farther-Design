Alternative investments · why and how
Benefits of investing in alternative assets
Diversification, return potential, and access to private markets
It is not sufficient to simply provide access. At Farther, we go above and beyond to ensure that our clients have top-tier, vetted options across alternative asset classes.
Diversification
Investments in alternative investment funds add diversification to your portfolio, improving its risk-return profile.
High-return potential
Alternative investments have historically provided one of the most attractive, risk-adjusted returns among asset classes.
Access to private markets
Investors in some alternative asset classes gain exposure to a carefully selected group of non-listed companies with potentially strong growth potential.
