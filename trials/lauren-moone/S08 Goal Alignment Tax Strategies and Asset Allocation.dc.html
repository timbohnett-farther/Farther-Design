Goals, taxes, and allocation
Three pillars
Goal alignment, taxes, and asset allocation
We are fiduciaries for our clients: we do not sell products to you, but make every choice for you based on your best interests.
Goal alignment
Investment decisions based on when you will need to access your assets for your specific goals, with no charges per account and no limit on the number of accounts you can set up
Proprietary technology for automatic account funding, allowing for dollar cost averaging, not timing the market, and staying the course with your goals
Taxes
We prioritize after-tax returns and focus on limiting the taxes from an income perspective, as well as short-term capital gains.
Tax-efficient transition tools to minimize the tax cost for new clients with existing portfolios
Multiple tax-efficient, customized direct indexing strategies and tax loss harvesting to enhance after-tax returns
Integration of asset allocation with asset location: tax-efficient investments in taxable accounts, and less efficient strategies in tax-deferred accounts

Asset allocation and reference
Asset allocation
We believe that asset allocation is about your goals and when you need your money. Whether short, intermediate, or long-term, we are committed to finding the best solution for your particular timing.
The ability to invest across the full spectrum of asset classes: starting with ETFs, with the ability to add, when appropriate, an alternative investment, an external fund manager, or exposure to commodities
Making every effort to achieve maximum diversification across asset classes, which has historically been proven to be the best market performance strategy
Technology for dynamic portfolio management: monitoring your portfolio and reallocating based on market changes
100% unbiased: insights derived from the world's largest asset manager, with no proprietary investment products
