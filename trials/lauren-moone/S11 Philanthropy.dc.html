Philanthropy through planning
Trust and estate planning
Part of a good philanthropic strategy is to identify how you can leave the assets to the causes you care about while also taking advantage of tax strategies. We can help you navigate the various options.
Certain trust accounts will determine how and when you receive the money. Would you prefer to donate over time, all at once, or sporadically?
A charitable remainder trust will provide income for your loved ones and then to your favorite charity
A charitable lead trust is irrevocable and will send the income to the charity for a period of time. Once that time has concluded, the grantor's heirs will receive the remaining funds

Appreciated stock, and rollovers to charity
Some of the best ways to donate to charity is through appreciated stock. This strategy allows you to gift stock to your favorite charities, and since they do not pay taxes, the organizations are able to keep the full amount. You will receive the full benefit of the donation and not have to pay capital gains taxes
You might be able to take those unneeded RMDs and roll them over to a charity, thereby both reducing your income and helping your favorite charities
By rolling over your tax-deferred retirement plan to a charitable organization as one of your beneficiaries, you will help your charity and also your heirs, since this strategy will reduce the amount of income or estate tax that they are responsible for
