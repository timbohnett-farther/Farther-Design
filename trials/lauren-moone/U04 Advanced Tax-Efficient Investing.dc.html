Tax planning
Keep more of what you already earn
Tax is the largest recurring cost most households at this level will ever pay, and the one cost that responds directly to structure. Two portfolios holding identical positions can differ by hundreds of thousands of dollars a year based only on which account holds what.
Most tax conversations start with which investment to buy. That is the last and least important decision. The order that produces the most value runs the other way: decide which account holds which asset, then harvest losses where they can be used, then select the vehicle.

Three points carry the piece
Asset location is the highest-value decision in tax-efficient investing and it costs nothing to implement. The QSBS rules changed materially in 2025, and for founders the gap between a year-three and a year-five exit can exceed two million dollars on a fifteen million dollar gain. And direct indexing remains the widest gap between an available tool and its adoption at this wealth level.
How Farther approaches this
Tax-efficient investing fails at the seams, where the advisor assumes the CPA is handling it and the CPA assumes the advisor is. Farther works alongside your existing tax and legal advisors rather than around them, and handles location and harvesting continuously rather than in a December scramble.
What that looks like in practice
Mapping every holding to the account type its tax character calls for, then executing in sequence
Tracking QSBS issuance dates and tier boundaries so exit timing is a decision rather than an accident
Harvesting losses through the year and banking them against future concentration reduction
Coordinating with your CPA before transactions close, not after the K-1 arrives
Modeling state conformity, since several states diverge on the provisions that matter most
