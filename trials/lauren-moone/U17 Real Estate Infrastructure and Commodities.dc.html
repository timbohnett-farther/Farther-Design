Real assets
Hard assets and the tax code that rewards them
The One Big Beautiful Bill Act permanently restored 100% bonus depreciation, which had been scheduled to fall to 20% in 2026 and disappear in 2027. Opportunity Zones became permanent in the same bill. Real assets are the one category where the tax code, the income, and the demand picture currently point the same direction.

The last two rows matter. This is acceleration, not elimination. Deductions are subject to passive activity loss limitations and are generally usable only against passive income unless the taxpayer qualifies as a real estate professional. Many investors will not be able to use the full deduction in year one.
What the $795,000 actually is
It is not a windfall. It is the family's own capital, returned early enough to do something with. In this illustration it funds roughly a quarter of the next acquisition. Component reclassification of 25% varies substantially by property type, and depreciation is recaptured on sale unless the property is exchanged or held until death.

Three points carry the piece
Real assets are three different exposures, and only two of them produce income. The tax treatment of directly held property is the most favorable in the code and just became permanent, which turns a deadline into a decision. And the infrastructure build is real, large, and already attracting more capital than discipline, which argues for structure and selectivity rather than enthusiasm.
How Farther approaches this
Real assets are where investment advice and tax advice stop being separable, and most firms are set up to give you only one. Farther coordinates directly with your CPA and attorney, and models depreciation, recapture, and exchange consequences before the transaction rather than after.
What that looks like in practice
Modeling the after-tax result, including whether passive activity rules will let you use the deduction
Coordinating cost segregation studies and exchange timing with your CPA so the sequencing holds together
Evaluating whether direct ownership, a fund, or a listed vehicle fits your liquidity and involvement preference
Sourcing infrastructure and real asset exposure through open architecture rather than a product shelf
Tracking recapture exposure across the portfolio so a future sale does not produce a surprise
