You should not have to wonder what someone is trying to sell you
Many people put off financial guidance out of a fear of pressure
Many people put off financial guidance because they worry a conversation will turn into pressure to buy a product, move money, or make a fast decision. Through Workplace to Wealth, you can start with a conversation about the question in front of you — your advisor explains your options, makes costs clear, and helps you decide what is worth doing next, if anything.

A best-interest standard
Your advisor is required to put your interests first when providing investment advice.
Clear fee conversations

Advice based on your situation
Keeping an account where it is can be the right answer. A rollover is never assumed.
Time to consider your options
You are not required to move money or decide anything during the meeting.
Written disclosures
You receive information about the relationship and compensation so terms are clear.
Fiduciary standard
A fiduciary advisor has a legal duty to act in your best interest when providing investment advice. For you, that means the conversation should start with your goals, your accounts, and your choices — not a product quota or a predetermined outcome.
